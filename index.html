Ask questions of more than a century of daily weather observations.

GHCN-Daily Chat answers questions about NOAA's Global Historical Climatology Network Daily record by finding stations, loading their observations and running the numbers in your browser. The language model runs through Lumen, the University of Illinois AI gateway, so you sign in there with your campus credentials and approve a key for this page.

The key is stored in this browser only. To stop it working, revoke it on your Lumen Profile page.

Built by Steve Nesbitt, Department of Climate, Meteorology & Atmospheric Sciences, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
